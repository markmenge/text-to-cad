# Mechanical Pattern: Prismatic Slider

Use when one part translates relative to another along a single axis.

- Parts: grounded guide and moving carriage/slider.
- Joint: prismatic.
- Axis: normalized translation direction.
- Limits: minimum and maximum travel in millimeters.
- Provide clearance between guide and slider surfaces.
- Use guide geometry that blocks the unwanted degrees of freedom while leaving intended travel possible.
- Keep guide and slider as distinct solids and validate one intended DOF.
